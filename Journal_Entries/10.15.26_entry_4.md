# Chapter 4 pt. 1
Knowing what to do: constraints, discoverability and feedback.

### General summary
This part of the chapter goes over constraints of discovering how something works- Physical, cultural, semantic, and logical.
Physical is what the physical thing can do- a driver has to face forward to see the road- and a wheel cannot be attached to nothing else it will roll away. 
Cultural could probably be attributed to social as well- how do you act when you enter an elevator? you don't start screaming and spinning around- you turn to face the directions and face the other way along with the others in the elevator.
Semantic works with the meaning of the situation - what works with what you know about the situation currently? What's your context? how does it apply? if you know the moped goes pretty quick- what do you think the windshield is for?
logical is what's left - if you know everything else - what does that last thing have to do?

In regards to the lego motorcycle- someone from Indonesia may look an realize its a type of bike- but not a becak because it doesn't have three wheels or 2 types of seats.
